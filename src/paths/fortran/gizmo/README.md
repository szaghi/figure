# The Fortran path's gizmos seam (szaghi/figure#15 P14)

The 3-D view's view cube and gizmos (`view3d_object%view_cube`, `view3d_object%gizmo`) and the gradient editor
(`ig_gradient_edit`) over ImGuizmo on the current path (`ViewManipulate`, `Manipulate`, `ImGradient::Edit`, FIGURE's
three patches in `third_party/ImGuizmo/patches/`, the glue in `native/figure_view3d.cpp` and
`native/figure_colormap.cpp`), here in Fortran on `figure_core`. The view itself (its software rasterizer, its camera,
picking) stays native: the gizmos read its widget, matrices and camera through the native view and tell it what they
did (`fig_view3d_widget`, `fig_view3d_matrices`, `fig_view3d_camera`, `fig_view3d_overlay`).

The seam of the view's gizmos is `figure_view3d_gizmos` (`fig_view3d_cube`, `fig_view3d_gizmo`, what
`src/lib/figure_view3d.F90` calls): on the current path the native layer's (`src/paths/imgui/gizmo/`), here
figure_gizmo's under the same names, arguments, statuses and messages. The gradient editor is the plot seam's
`ig_gradient_edit` (`src/paths/fortran/plot/figure_implot.F90`), drawn by `figure_gradient_edit`; an edit resamples the
map in the Fortran frame's registry (`figure_plot_colormaps`), the only one on this path: the 3-D view's colouring
reads its tables from it too (`fig_view3d_colormap_hooks`, installed by the plot seam's `figure_plot_hooks`).

Progress, and what is left, are tracked on [#15](https://github.com/szaghi/figure/issues/15) (P14), not here.

## Modules

- `figure_gizmo_math`: ImGuizmo's `vec_t` and `matrix_t` (and its free `Frustum`, `Perspective`, `LookAt`, `Cross`,
  `Dot`, `Normalize`) in single precision, operation by operation: sums in ImGuizmo's order, parenthesised; libm's
  `sinf`, `cosf`, `tanf`, `acosf`, `atan2f`, `fmodf` where ImGuizmo calls them. ImGuizmo has no quaternion: the view's
  camera is an azimuth and an elevation (native), the 3-D plots' quaternions are figure_plot3d_camera's.
- `figure_gizmo`: ImGuizmo's context (`gContext`, module variables), `Manipulate` (translate, rotate, scale; world or
  local; snap), `ViewManipulate` (FIGURE's patch 0002), the ID stack, `BeginFrameState` (patch 0001), the style; and
  the glue of `fig_view3d_cube` and `fig_view3d_gizmo` (`gz_view_cube`, `gz_view_gizmo`): the camera turned into the
  cube's y-up world and back, the world moved to the camera's target, the depth range widened, the pointer's "no
  mouse" position guarded, Dear ImGui's active ID held during a drag. What FIGURE does not ask of ImGuizmo is left out
  (orthographic cameras, the axis mask, bounds, the universal scale, grids, cubes); its defects are kept, so both draw
  the same (a gizmo behind the camera leaves its clip rectangle pushed; one `overBox` for every cube).
- `figure_view3d_gizmos`: the seam (above).
- `figure_gradient_edit`: `fig_gradient_edit` with `ImGradient::Edit` (patch 0003: the selection and the stop moved
  kept per editor in the window's storage), its float arithmetic kept.

They draw through `figure_core` only. The nvfortran `-fortran` modes build without FMA (`-Mnofma`), as the native
layer (`-ffp-contract=off`). No heap allocation per frame (`figure_test_gizmo_alloc`): nvfortran 26.9 makes a heap
temporary of every array expression given as an actual argument (`f(a - b)`, `f(-a)`, `f(a * s)`, even of 4
elements) and of a function result whose size is not a constant, not of a fixed-size function's result: figure_gizmo
passes `vsub(a, b)`, `vneg(a)`, `vmul(a, s)`, `vadd(a, b)` (figure_gizmo_math, the same arithmetic), never `trim`.

## Tests

`src/tests/fortran/figure_test_gizmo_*.F90`, built by the `-fortran` modes: each case drives the current path's
widget (`figure_view3d_gizmos_ref`, `figure_implot_ref`: the current path's modules under other names, generated by
`scripts/gen_implot_ref.py`) and the Fortran one with the same scripted input, 40 frames apart, and compares, every
frame, the matrix, the camera or the stops to the bit, and pictures at `test_compare_image(threshold=2,
max_failed=0)`: `_manipulate` (the gizmos: 9 cases), `_cube` (the view cube: 6 cases), `_gradient` (3 maps); `_alloc`
(no heap allocation per frame on the Fortran path, the gizmos dragged, the cube dragged and clicked).
